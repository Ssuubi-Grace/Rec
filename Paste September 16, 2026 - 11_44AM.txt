Yes — if you want your HRMIS Recruitment Module to feel premium, you should not just compare with traditional HRMIS products. Compare against the best interaction patterns from Workday, Oracle, SAP, HiBob, BambooHR, Rippling, Zoho Recruit, Darwinbox, Personio, UKG, and even products like Linear, Notion, Stripe and Slack for usability.

The goal should be:

Enterprise capability without enterprise complexity.

That is where many large HR systems struggle. They can do a lot, but sometimes require too many clicks, dense pages, and heavy configuration. Your opportunity is to make yours feel simpler, faster and more human while retaining strong workflows, auditability and controls.
